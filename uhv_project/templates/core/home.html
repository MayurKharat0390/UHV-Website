The Foundation of Relationships

9 Core Human Values

"The essence of harmony lies in understanding these nine foundational feelings, guiding us from basic trust to universal love."

🛡️
Trust (Vishwas)

The foundational value; the assurance that the other person wants my happiness and prosperity.

⚖️
Respect (Samman)

Right evaluation; recognizing the other person as an individual with similar potential and purpose.

💞
Affection (Sneha)

The feeling of being related to the other; accepting the other as my relative.

🫂
Care (Mamta)

The feeling of responsibility and commitment to nurture and protect the body of the other.

🧭
Guidance (Vatsalya)

The feeling of responsibility and commitment to ensure Right Understanding in the other.

🙏
Reverence (Shraddha)

The feeling of acceptance of excellence in the other; recognizing greatness in others.

✨
Glory (Gaurav)

The feeling of acceptance for those who have made efforts for excellence in life.

🌸
Gratitude (Kritagyata)

The feeling of acceptance for those who have made efforts for my excellence.

🧡
Love (Prema)

The complete value; the feeling of being related to all human beings and nature.
